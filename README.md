# Scott Trust & Document Services: website

Amanda Scott's business website. It's one file, `index.html`, with no build step. Edit it and it updates.

## How to change it
- **Easiest:** open `index.html` on GitHub, click the pencil icon, edit the text, then click **Commit changes**. The live site updates in about a minute.
- **With Claude:** paste the file into Claude and say what you want changed, for example "add a section about pet trusts", then paste the result back.

## Before going public (after LDA registration)
1. Fill in every `[bracketed]` item: LDA registration number, county, expiration date, phone, email, business address, hours.
2. Delete the yellow "Draft" bar at the top of the page.
3. Remove `<meta name="robots" content="noindex,nofollow">` from `index.html`, and delete `robots.txt`.
4. Optional: connect a custom domain (Settings → Pages → Custom domain).

Until then, the site is a private preview. Search engines are told not to list it.

## Rules the page must keep
- The statement "I am not an attorney. I can only provide self help services at your specific direction." (Bus. & Prof. Code §6408.5)
- LDA name, number, county and expiration on the page (§6408)
- No claims of registration, bonding or insurance until they're true.
